## The question

What is the most durable QoS 1 a 10-node cluster carries in the 1.3.0 configuration? The
configuration: 2 copies, the segment log store, 50 ms elision, and `$share` prefer-local (the
default), with persistent consumers.

## The run

- **Run:** `.runs/knee-20261022T231630Z` (untracked), 2026-10-22 23:16 to 2026-10-23 00:58
  UTC, Hetzner `fsn1`.
- **Hosts:** 10 × CCX23 brokers (4 vCPU each, 40 vCPU) and 20 × CCX33 drivers, one
  provisioning. The rig tore them down and audited for leaks: 0 servers, empty state.
- **A site:** 600 QoS 1 publishers at 10 msg/s (6,000 msg/s, 200 B) into a `$share` group of
  20 persistent consumers, 60 s windows.
- **The load generator's ceiling:** 20 drivers host 8 one-vCPU containers each, 3 per site, so
  53 sites (318,000 msg/s) is the most they can offer.
- **Arms:**

  | arm | configuration | ladder (sites) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | the 1.3.0 configuration | 1 · 40 · 45 · 50 · 53 |
  | 2 | the log store with elision off | 1 · 45 · 50 |
  | 3 | the 1.3.0 configuration again (drift control and repeat) | 1 · 50 |

### What it shows

- **Certified:** 270,000 msg/s at p99 ≤ 100 ms (27k per node, 6,750 per vCPU).
- **Carried:** at least **318,000 msg/s** (31.8k per node, about 7,950 per vCPU), the whole offer
  at p99 ≤ 500 ms. That is the load generator's ceiling, not the cluster's; a ceiling needs more
  drivers.
- **The repeat matches.** Arm 3 at 50 sites delivered 300,023 against arm 1's 300,048 (0.01%),
  so the hosts did not drift.
- **What elision adds at this size.** The log store alone is close to keeping up at 10 nodes,
  so the gain is in latency and headroom rather than throughput:
  - p99 ≤ 100 ms against ≤ 500 ms at 45 sites;
  - at 50 sites, the whole offer with 7% of publishes late (0% on the repeat), against 99.4%
    with 19% late without elision.
- **What is left unmeasured.** The held deliveries that are written after all batch less well:
  the mean write is 21 ms at 89% elided, against 13 ms with every delivery written. That is a
  lead for the log store's group commit under elision.
