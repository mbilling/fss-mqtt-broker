# Per-node knee at 3, 5, 7 and 10 nodes — one provisioning

Campaign card. Env: [`knee-3-5-7-10.env`](knee-3-5-7-10.env). Driver:
[`482-per-node-knee.sh`](482-per-node-knee.sh). Method and harness safeguards
(forwarding canary, full-mesh wait, driver gate, bad-host swap, drift control):
[`482-per-node-knee.md`](482-per-node-knee.md). Diagnostic only.

## The question

Does per-node QoS 0 `$share` capacity stay flat from 3 to 10 nodes — and where is
each size's knee?

The 2026-09-26 run answered 5 → 7 with floors only: every rung passed to ~78k
msg/s per node at both sizes. This run extends the ladders to 120k/node so each
size can reach its knee, and adds 3 and 10.

## Design

- **One provisioning:** 10 × CCX23 brokers + 20 × CCX33 drivers (200 vCPU, 30
  servers — exactly the project quota). Arms 10 → 7 → 5 → 3 → 10 on prefixes of
  the same hosts.
- **Drivers scale with brokers: 2N per arm.** At a matched per-node rate every
  driver then carries the same load at every size (rate / 60 000 sites per
  driver): 3 containers on the busiest driver up to 60k/node, 6 above.
- **10 consumers per site** in one container, so every broker holds a local
  member at every size; predicted crossing 0%.
- **Ladders matched in per-node offer**, 30k → 120k per node in ~6k steps (the
  table is in the env). **Each ladder stops after 2 consecutive failing rungs**
  (`LANE_E_STOP_AFTER_FAILS=2`, the summarizer's own verdict) and then runs its
  control; the skipped rungs are listed in `laneE/ladder-stop.txt`, the only
  absences the crossing gate accepts.

## The measure

`C_N` = the highest total offered rate at which the N-node arm passes that rung
**and every rung below it** (settled, drained, p99 ≤ 1000 ms, not PUBLISHERS
LATE, no driver saturated, crossing certified ≤ 0.5% on every broker).

`E(N) = (C_N / N) / (C_3 / 3)` — per-node capacity at N relative to 3 nodes.

Example: if 3 nodes knee at 9 sites (270 000/s) and 10 nodes at 30 sites
(900 000/s), `E(10) = (900 000 / 10) / (270 000 / 3) = 90 000 / 90 000 = 1.00`.

Resolution: one rung is ~6k/node (≈ 6–10% of a knee near 80–100k/node), and 3
nodes' steps are 10k/node — so `E` is resolved to roughly ±0.1.

## Decision rules — declared before the run

| outcome | reading |
|---|---|
| `E(N) ≥ 0.90` for 5, 7 and 10 | per-node capacity is flat to 10 nodes on this workload |
| some `E(N) ≤ 0.80`, crossing ≈ 0%, `rx_skew` ≈ 1 | a genuine scale-out cost at that N — investigate before tuning the knee |
| `0.80 < E(N) < 0.90` | inside the ladder's resolution; refine around those knees |
| an arm with no failing rung | its `C_N` is a floor; `E(N)` is a lower bound |
| closing 10-node arm off the opening by > ~1% | the hardware drifted; the comparison is void |
| any arm's crossing gate fails | that arm is not the shape; do not read it |

## Also read per arm

`rx_skew` / `eff_nodes`; hub publish busy per broker and µs per publish (the
2026-09-26 run found the slow-hub brokers are the ones whose NIC-softirq core
saturates — watch whether the knee lands on them); busiest driver idle; the
failing rungs' flags (a DRIVER CORE SATURATED or PUBLISHERS LATE knee is a
harness limit, not a broker one).

## Cost

Worst case every ladder climbs to 120k/node: 14 + 14 + 14 + 10 + 3 rungs ≈ 55
rungs at ≤ 13.2 min worst case each (`budgets.py`), ~5–6 min typical — about 5 h
of fleet time plus provisioning, on 30 servers. The early stop cuts each ladder
one rung past its knee.

## 2026-09-26 first attempt — stopped by one failed poll

The 10-node arm formed (mesh settled in 59 s), passed its forwarding canary on
all 90 directed pairs and its driver gate on all 20 drivers, calibrated clean and
passed its 1-site rung. During the 10-site rung's drain, one of twenty drivers
(driver 2) returned nothing for one delivery poll — its consumer was receiving a
steady 30 000/s and the host answered the evidence capture afterwards — and
`lane_e_recv_total || die "incomplete drain poll"` ended the campaign. Teardown
audited to zero. Fixed: the steady gate retries each driver's scrape
(`LANE_E_POLL_TRIES`, 3), keeping each attempt's stderr; a drain poll that fails
is written to `drain.tsv` as `poll-failed`, cannot count as flat, and the drain
continues to its deadline (UNRESOLVED if it never converges) instead of ending
the run. Evidence: `.runs/knee-20260926T055443Z/1-n10/`.
