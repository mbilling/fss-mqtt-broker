# SOC 2 / ISO 27001 evidence map

**Verified against `v1.0.2` (2026-08-21).** ADR 0067 T3. **Read the frame
right**: SOC 2 reports and ISO 27001 certificates attest an *organization's*
controls — no software product "is SOC 2 compliant", this one included. What a
product can honestly do is make the customer's evidence collection trivial:
this file maps mqttd features to the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and ISO/IEC
27001:2022 Annex A controls they help the **customer** satisfy, and names the
**artifact an auditor can pull** for each. Rows without a pullable artifact
don't appear.

## What the customer still owns

Everything organizational: personnel, physical, vendor management beyond this
component, risk assessment, incident-response *process* (mqttd gives the trail
and the runbook hooks, not the process), business continuity beyond the
broker's own DR drill, and at-rest encryption of volumes ([THREAT-MODEL
§4](../THREAT-MODEL.md) states the boundary). An auditor asking "who approved
this change" is asking about the customer's pipeline, not the broker.

Corrections follow the repository rule: versioned, dated, re-stamped each
release (ADR 0067 T4).
